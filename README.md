# 🍢 Supreme Chops International — Gourmet Small Chops Web App

[![React](https://img.shields.io/badge/React-18.x-61DAFB?style=for-the-badge&logo=react)](https://reactjs.org/)

[![Tailwind CSS](https://img.shields.io/badge/Tailwind_CSS-3.x-38B2AC?style=for-the-badge&logo=tailwind-css)](https://tailwindcss.com/)

An ultra-responsive, high-converting React single-page web application engineered for **Supreme Chops International**, a premium gourmet finger-food and event catering business based in Ikoyi/Obalende, Lagos, Nigeria.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🍱 1. Interactive Gourmet Food Catalog
* **Touch-Enabled Swiping:** Mobile-first category tabs with CSS scroll snapping across Platters, Customize Pack, and Frozen Chops.
* **Custom Pack Builder:** Real-time item adding with animated flying particle feedback into the cart drawer.

### 🚚 2. Smart Lagos Distance & Delivery Pricing Matrix
* **Precision Geolocation Pinning:** Measures exact GPS distance from the central Obalende kitchen depot (6.438384, 3.414441).
* **8-Tier Distance Pricing Engine:**
  * 1 – 5 km: **₦1,500**
  * 6 – 10 km: **₦3,000**
  * 11 – 15 km: **₦4,500**
  * 16 – 20 km: **₦6,000**
  * 21 – 25 km: **₦7,500**
  * 26 – 30 km: **₦10,000**
  * 31 – 35 km: **₦13,000**
  * 36 – 40 km: **₦15,000**
  * > 40 km: Out-of-range prompt.

### 🛒 3. Multi-Step Checkout Wizard
* **Step 1: Order Manifest & Bank Copy:** Itemized list and official OPay details (7081241745 - AYINDE YUSUF BELLO).
* **Step 2: Recipient Target:** Toggle between *For Myself* or *For Someone Else*.
* **Step 3: Fulfillment & Address:** Doorstep Delivery vs. *Self Pickup (Depot)* for ₦0 fee.

### 📄 4. Image Invoice Generator & WhatsApp Dispatch
* **Image Receipt Download:** Client-side HTML5 Canvas JPEG generation with unique reference codes (#SC-XXXX).
* **Direct 1-Click WhatsApp Routing:** Routes formatted order text straight to WhatsApp.

### 🖼️ 5. Photo Gallery & Event Booking
* **Visual Gallery:** Lightbox showcase for small chops platters, kebabs, and event setups.
* **Hamburger Drawer:** Mobile slide-over navigation.
